# LineFlex — Market and Competitor Research Plan

**Status:** Initial desk-research framework  
**Project stage:** Research

## 1. Research question

Is there a sufficiently important workflow problem around manufacturing flexibility and second-source readiness that is not already adequately handled by existing supplier-management, sourcing, ERP, or manufacturing-planning products?

The project must not assume the answer is yes.

## 2. Problem areas to investigate

1. Production capacity visibility and changeover constraints
2. Ability to redirect production between lines or facilities
3. Identification of alternative suppliers
4. Supplier qualification and customer-specific approval
5. Evidence and document management
6. Coordination when a supplier or production plan must change

For each problem, research should capture the affected user, current workaround, cost or consequence, frequency, and available software alternatives.

## 4. Comparison framework

For each product, record whether the capability is:
- Available as a documented product feature
- Available through a service or implementation
- Not found in public documentation
- Unclear and requiring confirmation

Compare:
- Factory capability records
- Capacity and scenario planning
- Alternative supplier discovery
- Customer-specific qualification tracking
- Quality evidence and audit workflow
- Production transfer coordination
- Integrations with ERP/MES/procurement tools
- Target customer size and geography
- Pricing model, where publicly disclosed

Do not label a capability “missing” merely because it is not visible on a marketing page.

## 5. Evidence standard

Every market claim should include:
- Exact claim
- Original source and direct URL
- Publication date
- Geography and industry covered
- Sample size and methodology, if available
- Whether it is a survey finding, company claim, analyst estimate, or independent evidence
- Limitations

Prefer primary research, official reports, government data, and product documentation. Use media coverage as supporting context, not as the sole basis for a major claim.

## 6. Validation gates

Before building beyond a small prototype, answer:

1. Is the workflow painful and frequent enough?
2. Is there a clear user and budget owner?
3. Are existing tools insufficient, or is integration the better answer?
4. Can the MVP solve one narrow task with sample data?
5. Can sensitive manufacturing information be protected?
6. Is there a credible route to adoption without requiring a two-sided marketplace from day one?
